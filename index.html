SEYED M.R. IRAVANI
Books
Seyed Iravani is a professor of Industrial Engineering and Management Sciences in the McCormick School of Engineering and professor of Operations at the Kellogg School of Management (courtesy).  His research focuses on the applications of stochastic processes, game theory, social networks and queueing theory to the design and control of manufacturing, service operations systems, health care, supply chains, and non-profit systems focusing on improving their flexibility, coordination, and responsiveness. 

Professor Iravani has worked with a wide range of companies in different industries to improve performance,  including  GM, Ford, Motorola, GE, Northwestern Memorial Hospital, Chicago Food Depository, Facebook, and Roti Mediterranean Restaurants, among others. He has served on the editorial board of journals such as Operations Research, Management Science, Service Science, IIE Transactions, and Naval Research Logistics.
